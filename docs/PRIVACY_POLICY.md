# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 20, 2026
**Last Updated:** August 20, 2026

## 1. Information We Collect

### Information You Provide
- **Telegram username and chat ID** (for signal delivery)
- **Payment information** (processed securely; we do not store card details)
- **Communication messages** when you contact support

### Information Collected Automatically
- **Trading signal delivery logs** (timestamps, signal types, tiers)
- **System performance data** (for improving signal quality)
- **Subscription status and tier level**

### Information We Do NOT Collect
- We do not collect login credentials for your exchange accounts
- We do not access your exchange API keys (unless you voluntarily provide them for VIP auto-trading)
- We do not collect personal identification documents

## 2. How We Use Your Information

- Deliver trading signals to your Telegram account
- Manage your subscription and payment status
- Provide customer support
- Improve signal quality and system performance
- Send service-related notifications

## 3. Information Sharing

We do **not** sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties except:

- **Payment processors** — solely for transaction processing
- **Telegram** — to deliver messages to your account (Telegram's own privacy policy applies)
- **Legal requirements** — when required by law or to protect our rights

## 4. Data Security

- All data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest (AES-256)
- API keys and credentials are encrypted using Fernet encryption
- Access controls limit who can view your information
- We follow industry-standard security practices

## 5. Data Retention

- Signal logs are retained for 90 days for service improvement
- Subscription records are retained for 24 months for accounting
- Payment records are retained as required by law
- You may request data deletion by contacting support

## 6. VIP Auto-Trading Data

If you opt into VIP auto-trading:

- Your exchange API credentials are encrypted with Fernet encryption
- Credentials are only used to execute authorized trades
- Credentials are never shared with any third party
- You may request credential deletion at any time

## 7. Cookies and Tracking

We do not use cookies or tracking technologies beyond what is necessary for basic service operation.

## 8. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

## 9. International Users

The Service is operated from Nigeria. If you access from outside Nigeria, you consent to data transfer to Nigeria.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. Material changes will be communicated via Telegram. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
